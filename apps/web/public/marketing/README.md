# Landing page photography

Four photos feed the "Inside a Zanoba lesson" section. Drop files here and
list their paths in `PHOTOS` at the top of `src/components/landing/Showcase.tsx`,
in card order. Any slot left `null` keeps its drawn chalk mock, so the section
never breaks while photos are missing.

## Shooting / generation notes

The reference look: candid, warm, natural light — never posed stock. Subjects
are mid-activity and rarely look at the camera. Shallow depth of field, subject
sharp, background softly thrown out. Leave clear negative space in the **top
third** of every frame: the two-line heading sits there.

## Prompts

Paste into your image tool of choice. Add your own aspect-ratio flag.

**1 — A tutor with a face** (4:5)
> Candid documentary photograph of a university student sitting at a desk in a
> warm apartment at dusk, city lights soft and out of focus through the window
> behind them. They are mid-conversation with someone on their laptop screen,
> hands raised slightly as they explain something, genuinely engaged. Warm lamp
> light from the left, deep navy shadows. Shot on 50mm, f/1.8, shallow depth of
> field. Natural skin texture, no retouching, subtle film grain. Empty ceiling
> and wall space above the subject's head.

**2 — A shared board** (16:10)
> Over-the-shoulder photograph of a student at a bright kitchen table, laptop
> open showing a digital whiteboard with handwritten equations and a sketched
> graph. A pencil and a notebook of working sit beside the laptop. Soft morning
> daylight from a window on the right, warm neutral tones. Shot on 35mm, f/2,
> shallow depth of field, the screen sharp and the room soft. Clear space at the
> top of the frame.

**3 — A curriculum per subject** (16:10)
> Photograph of a person on a sofa with a laptop on their knees, screen showing
> a tidy list of course units with progress marks. Pale wooden floor, a plant
> softly blurred behind. Late afternoon daylight, warm and low contrast. Shot on
> 35mm, f/2.2. Relaxed, unposed, face partly out of frame. Uncluttered space
> along the top edge.

**4 — Between the lessons** (21:9)
> Wide candid photograph of a young woman sitting on a park bench in dappled
> late-afternoon sunlight, one earbud in, typing a question into a tutoring app
> on her phone. Green foliage thrown far out of focus behind her. Warm golden
> light, gentle lens flare. Shot on 85mm, f/1.8. She is looking down at the
> phone, half-smiling, absorbed. Generous empty space to the left of the frame.

## A note before you publish

If these are AI-generated, the people in them do not exist. That is fine for a
mood-setting marketing image, but don't pair them with a name and a quote — the
testimonials section is the place where a face implies a real person, and those
should be real customers or nobody. Some markets also expect AI imagery to be
disclosed.
